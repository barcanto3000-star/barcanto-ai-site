Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Barcanto AI operates creative production and publishing workflows for Barcanto-owned content. Barcanto Crosspost is an internal publishing support workflow used by authorized operators.

Information processed

Depending on the connected platform, the workflow may process account identifiers, OAuth access tokens, upload status, post metadata, captions, video metadata, thumbnails, and local package records needed to prepare or verify Barcanto-owned content.

How information is used

Information is used to operate authorized Barcanto workflows, verify connected accounts, prepare media packages, perform read-only API checks, and support approved upload or publishing actions.

Storage and sharing

Credentials and tokens are intended to be stored locally in restricted operator-controlled files. Barcanto AI does not sell personal information. Data is shared with connected social platforms only as needed to operate approved integrations.
